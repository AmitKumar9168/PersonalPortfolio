ABOUT ME

Identity Is The Problem I Enjoy Solving.

I am an Identity and Access Management (IAM) professional with 3.5+ years of hands-on experience working with Ping Identity technologies and enterprise identity management solutions.

My expertise is centered on Ping Identity Access Management (PingAM), Identity Management (PingIDM), and Directory Services (PingDS). I design, develop, customize, implement, and troubleshoot authentication and identity management solutions — turning a login screen into a reliable, secure journey every time.

Beyond the core platforms, I work regularly with custom authentication nodes, scripting, and API integrations, and I bring a practical understanding of IAM architecture, identity lifecycle management, authentication, authorization, and directory services — including cloud-based IAM with Ping Advanced Identity Cloud.
